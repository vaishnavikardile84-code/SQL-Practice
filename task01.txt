### 1. Employee Directory

HR wants a list containing only the **employee name, department, city, and email**.

Write the query.

---SELECT name, department, city ,email FROM Employee;

### 2. Salary Eligibility

A company wants to identify employees eligible for a special benefit.

Eligibility:

* salary is at least ₹40,000
* employee is active
* city is Pune

Display their **name, salary, and city**.

---SELECT * FROM Employee WHERE salary = 40000 AND Is_Active = True AND city = 'Pune';

### 3. Department Search

The HR team wants employees belonging to **IT, HR, or Finance**.

Use the most appropriate operator rather than writing multiple `OR` conditions.

---SELECT * FROM Employee WHERE IN Department ( 'IT' , 'HR' , 'Finance' ); 

### 4. Age-Based Recruitment

A company is hiring candidates between **21 and 28 years old**, including both ages.

Find those employees.

---SELECT * FROM Employee WHERE Age >= 21 AND Age <= 28 ;
---SELECT * FROM Employee WHERE IN Age ( 21 , 28 );
### 5. Employee Name Search

The HR employee-search system should find everyone whose name **starts with "R"**.

Use pattern matching.

--- SELECT * From Employee WHERE IN name like 'R%'; 

### 6. Email Investigation

The IT team wants to find employees whose email address **ends with `@gmail.com`**.

Write the query.

---SELECT * FROM Employee WHERE IN email like '%@gmail.com';

### 7. Salary Ranking

Management wants a salary report where the **highest-paid employee appears first**.

Display: 

```text
name

```

---SELECT * FROM Employee ORDER BY Salary limit 2 offset 1 AS ASC ;
OR
---Select name , department , salary FROM Employee ORDER BY Salary limit 2 OFFSET 1 AS ASC ; 

### 8. Top Performers

---SELECT * FROM Employee ORDER BY Salary LIMIT 3 AS ASC;
