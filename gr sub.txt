## 🔥 10 SQL Practice Questions

### GROUP BY

**1.** Show the **total quantity of products available in each category**.
-->  SELECT COUNT(Quantity) FROM Products;

**2.** Show the **average price of products for each category**.
-->  SELECT avg(Quantity) FROM Products;

**3.** Show the **maximum price in each category**, but display only categories where the maximum price is **greater than 3000**.
--> SELECT category from product where max(price) > 3000 GROUP BY category;  
-->SELECT * FROM Products WHERE category > ( SELECT MAX(price) > 3000);   

**4.** Show each **brand** and the **number of products** belonging to that brand.

**5.** Show the **total value of stock for each category**.
Stock value = `Price × Quantity`.
