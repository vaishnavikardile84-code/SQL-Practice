### 🏋️ Gym Management — 15 SQL Thinking Questions

1. The gym owner wants to see which members currently have an **active membership**. Display their names and membership types.
-> SELECT name , is_active FROM Gym_Management WHERE membership_status = 'Active';

2. The manager wants to identify the **3 members paying the highest monthly fees**.
->SELECT MAX(monthly_fees) FROM gym_members ORDER BY monthly_fees DESC limit 3; 

3. The marketing team wants to find members whose names **start with the letter R**.
->SELECT * FROM gym_members WHERE name like 'R%';

4. The accounts team wants to know the **average monthly membership fee**.
->SELECT AVG(monthly_fee) FROM gym_members;

5. The gym wants to find members who joined during **2026** and are from Pune.
->SELECT Joining_date , city FROM gym_member WHERE Joining_date = '2026' , City = 'pune';

OR 

->SELECT * FROM gym_members WHERE Joining_date = 2026 , City = 'pune';

6. The manager wants to see members whose age is **between 20 and 28**.
->SELECT * FROM gym_members WHERE age IN (20 AND 28);

7. The gym wants to know which **different cities** its members come from, without repeating a city.
-> SELECT DISTINCT(city) FROM gym_members;

8. A member has changed their city from Pune to Mumbai. Update that member's record accordingly.
->UPDATE gym_members SET city = 'Mumbai';

9. The gym wants to find members whose email address **ends with `@gmail.com`**.
->SELECT * FROM gym_members WHERE email like '%@gmail.com';

10. The manager wants to identify members who have either a **Gold or Premium** membership.
-> SELECT * FROM gym_members WHERE membership_type IN ('GOLD' OR 'Premium');

11. The gym wants to know the **total monthly revenue** from all active members.
->SELECT sum(monthly_fee) FROM gym_members WHERE membership_status = ('active');

12. The trainer wants to see active members from **Pune or Mumbai** whose monthly fee is above ₹2,000.
-> SELECT city FROM gym_members WHERE city IN ('PUNE' , 'Mumbai') AND monthly_fee > 2000; 

13. The manager wants to find the **youngest member** registered in the gym.
-> SELECT age FROM gym_members WHERE age IN ( 20 30 );

OR

SELECT age FROM gym_members WHERE age >= 20 AND age <= 30;

14. The gym wants to remove the record of a member who permanently cancelled their membership.

-> DELETE FROM gym_members WHERE membership_status = ('Permanently cancelled');
